# Strategic Business Recommendations

**Project:** E-Commerce Customer, Sales & Business Intelligence Analytics  
**Target:** Executive Leadership, Growth & Logistics Teams  

---

## 1. Regional Fulfillment & Logistics Optimization

| Initiative | Strategic Rationale | Implementation Plan | Expected Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Establish Northern & Northeastern Micro-Hubs** | Northern states suffer from 25–30 day transit times and 2.5x higher cancellation rates. | Partner with 3PL logistics hubs in Salvador (BA) and Recife (PE) to hold fast-moving SKUs. | Reduce transit time from 27d to <12d; increase regional CSAT from 3.6 to >4.2. |
| **Dynamic Delivery Date Buffering** | Delayed deliveries experience a 55% surge in 1-star ratings. | Adjust the estimated delivery calculation engine to add a 48-hour algorithmic safety buffer on inter-state shipments. | Decrease delay rate from 8.1% to <3.5%; protect platform brand reputation. |
| **Carrier SLA Scoring & Allocation** | Multiple carriers exhibit inconsistent fulfillment speed across rural zip codes. | Implement automated carrier performance scoring. Route orders dynamically to the highest-performing carrier per route. | Improve on-time fulfillment rate by 12–15%. |

## 2. Customer Retention & Repeat Purchase Engine

| Initiative | Strategic Rationale | Implementation Plan | Expected Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category Replenishment CRM Workflows** | 96.88% of customers are single-order buyers; top category is consumables (Health & Beauty). | Build automated email/SMS workflows triggered 30, 45, and 60 days post-delivery with 1-click reorder incentives. | Lift repeat customer rate from 3.12% to 6.50% within 12 months. |
| **Second-Order Voucher Program** | CAC is fully absorbed on the first order; second orders deliver immediate margin expansion. | Offer R$ 20 credit valid on orders over R$ 100 within 30 days of first successful delivery. | Increase 60-day customer re-engagement rate by 25%. |
| **VIP Spender Tier (Orders > R$ 300)** | Top 4.6% of customers generate 27.1% of gross platform revenue. | Introduce free shipping and priority customer support for verified high-value accounts. | Boost high-value customer LTV by 18%. |

## 3. Product Catalog & Commercial Strategy

| Initiative | Strategic Rationale | Implementation Plan | Expected Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category Consolidation (Pareto Focus)** | Top 15 categories generate 78.4% of revenue, while bottom 20 categories generate under 1.1%. | Focus co-marketing budgets and merchant acquisition on top 5 categories (Health & Beauty, Watches, Bed & Bath, Sports, Computers). | Improve merchandising return on ad spend (ROAS) by 22%. |
| **Freight Bundling & Minimum Spend Thresholds** | Freight costs exceed 30% of order value in low-ticket items, causing checkout abandonment. | Implement "Free Shipping on 2+ items" or "Spend R$ 150 to unlock 50% off shipping". | Increase Average Order Value (AOV) from R$ 159 to R$ 185. |

## 4. Payment Gateway & Financing Strategy

| Initiative | Strategic Rationale | Implementation Plan | Expected Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0%-Interest Installment Campaigns** | Credit card represents 75.4% of payments, with an average of 3.5 installments. | Negotiate subsidized multi-installment processing fees with card acquirers during Q4 promotional peaks. | Increase checkout conversion by 8–10% on high-ticket goods (>R$ 300). |
| **Boleto Expiration Follow-up Automation** | Boleto payments (17.3% share) face high abandonment due to unpaid invoices. | Send automated WhatsApp reminders with payment barcodes 24h before boleto expiration. | Reduce boleto payment drop-off rate by 15%. |
